What the fixtures exercise

  • deterministic transition guards and human approvals;
  • provider uncertainty, retries, idempotency, and reversals;
  • bank reconciliation, invoices, e-documents, expenses, collections, exchange rates, ledger workflows, and organization authorization;
  • reconstructible audit events and user-visible status aligned with source-of-truth state.

Controlled-automation principles

  • An AI recommendation is decision support, not a committed accounting fact.
  • Approval does not prove that an external financial operation was committed.
  • An unavailable provider result is unknown and must not be presented as a negative result.
  • Retries must not duplicate committed financial effects.
  • Reversals and corrections remain explicit, auditable state transitions.
Responsible use. These records are illustrative test fixtures, not production-ready workflows and not accounting, tax, legal, or investment advice. They contain no real customer data, bank data, invoice or taxpayer identifiers, production model output, provider secrets, or live accounting records.
